# The review handoff
Date: 2026-10-08

The CRO assesses one book, one holdings date and one eligible Evidence publication. `alphalattice review continue --dir "<out>/review"`, after the Analysts' answers, writes the CRO's bundle: it reads current Evidence, then its `dossier`, then the dossier's `cro_bundle`. Settle any Evidence continuation first ([Evidence handoff](evidence-analysis-handoff.md)); a CRO run before it reads a publication the continuation replaces. If no bundle is offered, follow the answer's action or report its held reason; never rebuild a bundle from edited artifacts.

Give the CRO only the bundle path, file list and answer file, never your interpretation; its source exposure and budget are those the goal declared or a first use delegated. On `CORRECT`, the same reviewer fixes only the named issues, at most twice, never changing judgment, book or reviewer. `ACCEPTED` admits the answer; after the second correction, `DONE` admits acceptable risks and records the rest as dropped. Keep the receipt, not the answer. `alphalattice review continue --dir "<out>/cro" --package <package>` then publishes the review and reads the activation offer; on a dated update, follow its offered next action, never the daily setting: `CONVENE_THE_COMMITTEE` (on the first use's date, and on a later date with the person's daily committee setting on) convenes the committee and its report; `REPORT_THE_COMMITTEE` once its floor has closed; `GIVE_THE_PM_VIEW` (a later date with the setting off) is your view as PM from the Host's materials, the committee offered in one line. Once that review is published, `alphalattice research-update show --task <task>` offers the same committee, and so does your continue on an earlier bundle refused as stale or ambiguous after a second preparation; a passing refusal, such as a refresh still running, keeps its own way. A separate role's judgment is not independent validation; unknown external tokens are unavailable, not zero.
