# Requests and capability routes
Date: 2026-10-10

Use this index when the person's request is not already an answer's offered next step. Start from the relevant controls or read, then follow its `next_action`, `next_commands` and `next_templates`; do not manufacture a field or edit product source to obtain a missing capability. The [operating contract](operating.md) holds command syntax, saved answers and waits; the [research contract](research-contract.md) holds evidence and claims.

The routes below identify every command family. Their `--help` forms inspect the accepted request; use help only for a form or field the answer leaves unclear, not as a script to run before each step. Reads and plans do not authorize a run, confirmation or activation. Reads, local exports and requested Task cancellation are the agent's actions; a separate prerequisite decision keeps its own authority. Ask only for a currently required person-owned decision; discover ids and take and disclose defaults yourself. Put future permission conditions in the relevant later step. For a person-only request, prepare the exact sendable command from its readback, ask once in plain words and relay the actual answer through `person_confirmation`; a delegated decision is disclosed rather than asked again.

## Change a declaration, not its kept result

For a study copy, obtain its declaration from controls or a draft, edit only admitted fields, and plan it before running. A new input or numerical method requires new evidence; never rewrite a completed result. Generated hashes, resolved listing axes and frozen recipe outputs are not caller choices. Select handles only from the controls or existing parents.

The fields below are grouped by the declaration that accepts them. Bounds, defaults and installed choices come from that declaration's schema and controls, not from an imagined option. Study ranges and a research cutoff change the evaluated research; they do not change each historical trading clock. Candidate and numerical-call budgets bound admission; seed and scientific parameters define the experiment. The authored thread limit is recorded in the envelope, whereas `cpu-budget` is the operator's execution setting. For an input-bound study, schema_id is fixed and output and baseline locations are owner-managed record fields, not free choices.

Direct model-parameter studies support fixed-iteration training; early stopping without a tuning partition is refused at plan. Changing model refit interval, anchor month, training window, purge, seeds, vintage weights or score aggregation is supported by the lifecycle declaration; this is not a change to trading cadence. Risk controls offer estimator choices and their admitted decay, blend or shrinkage parameters; the installed lookbacks remain fixed. A registered universe profile or deterministic sample is not an arbitrary supplied ticker list, and a sample is not admitted to every study kind. A requested three-year replay must first fit the book's kept support; a report slice cannot manufacture missing history.
