# Pipeline issues
Date: 2026-10-07

Use this guide when preparation or an update stops for data review, or when checking input coverage. Start from the exact Task and owner answer; a failure code alone is not a root cause. Review decisions through their offered path, not through code repair or a second quality policy.

## Read the decision

- `decision list` shows each pending step and whom it `waits_on`: the `PERSON`'s, or yours (`AGENT`) to take and disclose. An `INPUT_VERSION` step waits on the person: keep the research on its current input and tell them that newer data can become a new input version on **Research inputs**. `issue list --section case_index` lists current cases; read each whole case and its evidence, prior decisions, current options, consequences, confirmable ids and `next_requests`. A listed option or compact omission is not proof it can be used now.
- A large raw move alone proves neither corruption nor a real event. Distinguish a raw move, invalid adjusted prices, missing sessions and identity mismatch; do not invent corporate action or news.
- For quarantine or exclusion, state the retained history and effective population. Keep adverse observations and the requested universe; never filter to improve results.
- A refusal asking for a full-history audit or a catch-up beyond a year names its listings: renew the update or preparation plan admitting them and take it under the plan's authority, saying so in one line. It is work scope, not a person's stop.
- Use only an owner-offered bounded retry or wait. Expiry permits reassessment, not clearance. A Data specialist recommends; the product and person decide.

## Preview, confirm and continue

1. Take the current exact preview request from `next_requests`; never rebuild its case, evidence or hashes.
2. Preview validates but applies nothing. `CONFIRMATION_REQUIRED` and `effect_applied: false` are not success. If evidence or case changed, read the current issue and reassess.
3. A person confirms a data issue. For the admitted first preparation and its scoped data issues, an agent under the workspace's open `FIRST_USE` goal may confirm within 24 hours of its original opening; the Host records the person's delegation and the actual agent session. Under that goal, take an offered `retain_isolated_raw_move_with_caveat` for an isolated verified move and disclose its caveat, and confirm the update plan's membership changes; membership follows its source. Outside that delegation, automation needs the person's exact grant and keeps its own actor provenance. Activation outside the goal's reviewed book, storage, automation settings and revocation remain the person's.
4. Read the applied effect and refreshed issues, then follow the original Task's offered continuation. Use `RECOVER` only when recovery is required. Keep valid stages and source captures; never recreate a workspace to escape a stop.
5. Read the continuing Task and current readiness and input publication. A receipt, `CONFIRMED_PENDING_REVALIDATION` or `NO_PENDING_CASE_CATALOG` alone does not prove a usable input; more cases may remain.

If the same listings return, compare evidence and manifest or qualification bindings before calling a retry failed. A baseline-Feature qualification may create a manifest from unchanged prices; an old decision does not cover a new case. If scope and evidence did not change or decisions repeat without progress, record a reproducible product issue instead of confirming again.

## Read the research clocks

Read `timing` from the relevant section of the saved answer; never run research just to inspect its clocks. `declared.as_of` is the cutoff with a market phase: a date alone does not permit post-close data. `input` is the immutable observation range; `evaluated` is formation support, and neither means every date trained. Sealed Panel availability records declared phase and delay, not measured delivery or point-in-time proof; missing history stays `NOT_RECORDED`. Read the outcome method, target maturity, folds, purge and lifecycle: one-session outcomes, quarterly refits and Risk diagnostics do not imply daily trading. Portfolio `selected_event` gives formation and real decision/open/end times; do not rebuild them from calendar days. Outcome end is not liquidation.

When clocks do not show an input was usable, state what is unknown and follow the owner's refusal. Do not change formula lag, backdate publication or assume live execution. Keep candidates, nominal membership, raw readiness, Feature eligibility and effective population distinct. Read PLAN's range, consumed formations and holdout before RUN; do not open held-out periods to match labels. Reopen an exact non-current Foundation admission instead of rebuilding or activating it.

Keep case/Task locators, user choices, receipts and open questions in authorized local notes. Shared guidance holds only source-checked reusable procedures; keep private cases, symbols, payloads, credentials and machine paths out.
