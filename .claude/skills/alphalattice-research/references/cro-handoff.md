# The review handoff
Date: 2026-10-08

The CRO assesses one book, one holdings date and one eligible Evidence publication. `review continue`, after the Analysts' answers, writes the CRO's bundle: it reads current Evidence, then its `dossier`, then the dossier's `cro_bundle`. Settle any Evidence continuation first ([Evidence handoff](evidence-analysis-handoff.md)); a CRO run before it reads a publication the continuation replaces. If no bundle is offered, follow the answer's action or report its held reason; never rebuild a bundle from edited artifacts.

Give the CRO only the bundle path, file list and answer file, never your interpretation; its source exposure and budget are those the goal declared or a first use delegated. On `CORRECT`, the same reviewer fixes only the named issues, at most twice, never changing judgment, book or reviewer. `ACCEPTED` admits the answer; after the second correction, `DONE` admits acceptable risks and records the rest as dropped. Keep the receipt, not the answer. `review continue --dir "<out>/cro" --package <package>` then publishes the review and reads the activation offer. A separate role's judgment is not independent validation; unknown external tokens are unavailable, not zero.
