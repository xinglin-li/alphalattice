# Work with research agents
Last checked: 2026-10-22 against 0.2.3

Claude Code or Codex leads your research; AlphaLattice calculates, validates and
records it. The installing agent continues in the same session, and a session
binds itself when it first works on the workspace. The [agent guide](../../AGENTS.md)
owns setup, delegation and person decisions. Its
[Research Skill](../../.agents/skills/alphalattice-research/SKILL.md) owns research
procedures. On Claude Code, the first Host start can ask the person to run the
offered command once in their own terminal. That startup act is separate from
research and network decisions; see [Getting started](getting-started.md).

## Specialists and evidence

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Data | Explain a data issue and its permitted choices |
| Factor | Interpret screening evidence and curation |
| Alpha | Interpret candidates, folds and predictive support |
| Risk | Interpret diagnostics and coverage |
| Portfolio | Interpret product-calculated books and compatible comparisons |
| Evidence Analyst | Submit cited issuer findings from its prepared bundle |
| CRO | Separately challenge the selected book against those findings |

Specialists interpret AlphaLattice's prepared material. Their judgment grants
no calculation, publication or activation authority. Read each result's five
standing fields: `comparison`, `execution`, `contract`, `evidence` and
`activation`. Their statements explain each mark; a completed Task alone
establishes no scientific correctness or activation. [Research flows](research-flows.md)
covers prerequisites and claims. [Extending](extending.md) covers trials and activation.

In the committee, initial stances stay blind until they are revealed. Each specialist then uses its discussion turn with a `CHALLENGE` or `REPLY`, or yields it with `PASS`. A pass names no target or position and ends that specialist's participation. The existing cap is three challenges or replies per specialist in all; a pass does not count toward it. The portfolio manager rules on every challenge. The floor is settled once every specialist has spoken or passed and no challenge remains unruled, or once its short discussion window after the reveal has passed: a specialist silent past it has passed (silent), and a challenge still unruled is reported as unruled; the portfolio manager can then give the verdict. A time-box close can still leave a member unaddressed. The verdict is the committee's discussion record, not an approval or advice: `PROCEED_WITH_NOTES` by default, with reservations carried as notes; a problem with particular holdings is a note and a suggestion to avoid or reduce them, never a `DECLINE`; `DECLINE` only for a named book-level hard defect (a data error affecting the book, a breached book Risk bound, or key evidence missing for the book as a whole); `FOR_THE_PERSON` only for a named decision of the person. Members may suggest avoiding, reducing or adding specific holdings, with reasons from the materials. The report leads with those suggestions, then the verdict, and ends by saying that the committee changed no number and that whether to act on its suggestions is the person's decision.

## Goals and Team

**Goals** records the objective, criteria, deliverables, bounds and unresolved
work. The Host checks the submission's shape and bindings: declared criteria,
deliverable references, Task states and open problems. It does not judge an
interpretation's truth. Follow the goal's **Timeline**, **Conversation** and **Results**.

A Codex Task's registered wake is kept by the Host; Claude Code provides
background completion notices.

**Team** and **Conversation** show recorded requests, prepared bundles, accepted
answers, decision notes and observed usage. An Evidence Analyst or CRO submits
its own answer with its bundle's key and is recorded as its author; a stage role's
answer the lead submits stays `NOT_OBSERVED`. Prepared bundles awaiting answers
appear as open assignments and do not block submission. Usage shows each member's
latest reading separately. [Privacy](privacy.md) explains the reader and
controls; raw conversation and private reasoning are not published.
