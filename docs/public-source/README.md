# AlphaLattice research workstation
Date: 2026-10-08

Give your Codex or Claude Code agent one research intent and the repository link.
It installs AlphaLattice and continues in the same session. You can follow Tasks,
results and reviews in Local Web. [Getting started](getting-started.md) covers
first use. The [agent guide](../../AGENTS.md) owns procedures and person decisions.

AlphaLattice calculates local equity research and records its evidence. Studies
report historical research; an activated installed strategy can publish dated
forward research positions. [Research flows](research-flows.md) explains their
claims and limits. AlphaLattice executes no trades and promises no future returns.

## Read results within their scope

Read each result's standing, input, method, coverage and temporal statement.
Membership, Sector backfill, survivorship and price basis limit its claims.
Command success and publication establish no scientific endorsement.
