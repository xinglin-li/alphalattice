# AlphaLattice Governance
Date: 2026-10-08

Xinglin Li created AlphaLattice and is its sole final decision maker. The
maintainer decides the project's direction, contribution acceptance, releases
and governance. Only Xinglin Li holds repository administrator access.

Contributor pull requests require the Verify checks (`Source checkout` and
`Installed wheel`), the CLA check and the maintainer's CODEOWNERS approval.
Branch protection does not enforce its rules on administrators: the sole
maintainer keeps the administrative bypass for direct exports to `main`.

The maintainer appoints and removes other maintainers and grants permissions
for their assigned responsibilities. Contributors may earn these permissions
through their accepted work; appointment does not transfer final authority.

Merged pull requests receive credit through their preserved authorship and
public pull request record. Contributions follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)
and the [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md).

The project may move to an organisation later; links will redirect.
