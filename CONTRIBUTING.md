# Contributing to AlphaLattice
Date: 2026-10-01

The first release takes no outside contributions.

Others may join after the first release once the ownership of the existing
work, the rights to new contributions, credit and permissions are agreed.

Public verification runs `scripts/check_playpen.py --all-python --fast` and
`scripts/build_local_web_ui.py --product --check`; full and real-evidence gates
require the maintainers' private checkout and refuse when its inputs are absent.
